Responsibilities

Talent is the voice. Influence Mobile runs the business.

Talent

Bring authentic fan connection.
  • Introduce the club.
  • Share the club and games they enjoy in their own voice.
  • Keep fans engaged on an agreed schedule.
  • Optionally provide special rewards or fan experiences.

Influence Mobile

Run everything else.
  • Build and operate the app.
  • Fund and fulfill rewards.
  • Handle customer support.
  • Manage servers, licensing, privacy and compliance.
  • Bring game-publisher relationships.
  • Fund the minimum guarantee.
